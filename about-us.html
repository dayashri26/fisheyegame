About Us

Matsya-Vedh is an independent, browser-based game project. It was built as a small tribute to one of the most memorable trials of skill in Indian mythology — the fish-eye test from the Mahabharata — reimagined as a playable archery game with a real-time water-reflection mechanic.

What we make

Right now, that's this one game and the accompanying story pages that explain its mythological background. The goal has been to treat the source legend with care — telling it accurately across several short chapters — while building an original, modern interactive experience around it rather than a literal reconstruction.

Our approach

Everything on this site runs directly in your browser. There's no account system, no downloads, and — as explained in our Privacy Policy — no cookies or on-device storage. The game and its story content are free to play and read.

Matsya-Vedh is designed and maintained by a small independent team with a shared interest in Indian mythology and browser-based game design. We build and update this project in our own time, and every feature — from the reflection-aiming mechanic to the story chapters — is written and reviewed in-house.
