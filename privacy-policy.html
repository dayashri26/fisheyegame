Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This policy explains what data Matsya-Vedh ("the game", "this site") collects, why, and who it may be shared with. This game is a free, browser-based archery game inspired by Indian mythology.

1. Data we do not collect

This site does not require an account, does not use cookies, and does not use local storage to track you or store anything on your device. Gameplay data (score, level, arrows) exists only in your browser's memory while the page is open, and is cleared the moment you close or refresh the tab.

2. Fonts and technical rendering

This page renders entirely using fonts already installed on your device or operating system. No font files, scripts, or other assets are loaded from an external service to display this page.

Your device's timezone and browser language are also used to decide which page title to display ("Matsya-Vedh" or "Aim the Fish Eye") — this happens entirely inside your own browser and is never sent anywhere.

3. Advertising

This site may display advertisements served through third-party advertising partners. If and when ads are shown:

  • Our advertising partners and their own partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • Ad data may be used to personalize the ads you see, subject to your consent settings where required by law.
  • You can manage or limit ad personalization at any time through your browser's privacy settings, your device's ad-tracking controls, or by using an ad-blocking or cookie-blocking extension.
If you are visiting from the European Economic Area or UK, ads (once enabled) will only be personalized after you provide consent, in line with applicable data protection law.

4. Children's privacy

This game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your choices

You can use browser extensions or settings (such as "Do Not Track", ad blockers, or cookie blockers) to limit third-party data collection — this will not affect core gameplay.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves (for example, if advertising is enabled). The "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to us via our Contact Us page.
