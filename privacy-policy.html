Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This policy explains what data Matsya-Vedh ("the game", "this site") collects, why, and who it may be shared with. This game is a free, browser-based archery game inspired by Indian mythology.

1. Data we do not collect

This site does not require an account, does not use cookies, and does not use local storage to track you or store anything on your device. Gameplay data (score, level, arrows) exists only in your browser's memory while the page is open, and is cleared the moment you close or refresh the tab.

3. Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. If and when ads are shown:

  • Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • Google may use data collected to personalize ads, subject to your consent settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or generally by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
If you are visiting from the European Economic Area or UK, ads (once enabled) will only be personalized after you provide consent, in line with Google's EU User Consent Policy.

4. Children's privacy

This game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your choices

You can decline the on-page notice on the homepage to prevent the country-lookup request from being made. You can also use browser extensions or settings (such as "Do Not Track", ad blockers, or cookie blockers) to further limit third-party data collection — this will not affect core gameplay.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves (for example, if advertising is enabled). The "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to us via our Contact Us page.
